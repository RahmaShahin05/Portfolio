About Me

Who am I?

I’ve always been curious about the story behind numbers — how data can explain customer behavior, business performance, and even predict better decisions. That curiosity is what led me to start my journey in Data Analysis.

Through my technical training at the National Telecommunication Institute (NTI), I gained hands-on experience working with tools like Power BI, Tableau, Excel, and Python to transform raw data into clear insights and interactive dashboards.

What excites me most about data analytics is not just building charts or reports, but helping businesses truly understand what their data is trying to say — where opportunities exist, what challenges need attention, and how better decisions can be made with confidence.

I’m continuously improving my analytical and problem-solving skills to create data-driven solutions that are practical, impactful, and easy to understand.

My goal is to turn data into meaningful stories that help businesses grow smarter and make stronger decisions.
